Features

Everything on the home screen.

Six ways to move, one tap away. Pick one to see what it does.

Exercise snacks

One move between meetings, after a focus round, while the kettle boils.

  • Bite: one move, under a minute.
  • Plate: about five minutes of guided work.
  • À la carte: set your own duration, sets and rest.
  • Picked at random so it stays fresh. Swap the exercise before you start.
  • Every snack move has an animated figure showing how to do it.

Build your own workout

Pick the length. IpsoFit builds the rest.

  • Choose the duration, focus and intensity.
  • Full body, upper body, lower body or core.
  • Mixes EMOM, Tabata, AMRAP, HIIT circuits and supersets.
  • Optional warm-up and cooldown.
  • Wherever you are: bodyweight only when you are out, or the equipment you have at home or the gym. Tell it where you are and it picks only moves you can do.
  • Review the plan, swap a move or regenerate it.

Mobility sessions

Joints and stretching, start to finish.

  • Choose the duration and the emphasis: full body, upper body, lower body or core.
  • Uses only the equipment you have, or none at all.
  • Generate the plan, then start the session.

Stretch a muscle

Tap the area that feels tight. Get a short routine for it.

  • Front and back body map: tap the muscle you want to stretch.
  • Two or three movements, built for that muscle.
  • Set the duration and the rest between movements, or leave them on auto.

Eye rest

Short exercises for screen-heavy days.

  • A guided block you can start any time from the home screen.
  • Moves such as figure-eight tracing, with a timer that paces you.
  • Pair it with an eye-rest nudge so you do not have to remember.

Nudges

Reminders to move and to rest your eyes, on your schedule.

  • Snack nudge: every few minutes, a nudge to get up and do an exercise snack.
  • Eye-rest nudge: a reminder to look into the distance for 20 seconds.
  • Pick the interval, line nudges up with the clock, and snooze when you are busy.
  • Limit them to your working hours if you like.

And more

Small details that keep you moving.

Focus timer

A built-in Pomodoro timer runs focus rounds with short breaks. A break can end with an exercise snack.

Stats and body map

Today and this week: blocks, sets, workouts, and the muscles you worked on a front and back body map.

Works with what you have

Tell IpsoFit your equipment and it picks only moves you can do. Bodyweight only works in an office, a hotel room or a park.

Backup and restore

Your history stays on your device. Export it to a backup file and restore it whenever you like.

Light or dark, English or Spanish

Follow your system or pick a theme and a language yourself.

A library of 600+ exercises

Every muscle group, from bodyweight to gym equipment. IpsoFit draws from all of it, so your sessions stay varied.

Questions

Good to know

Which devices does IpsoFit run on?

Android phones and Windows PCs. The app is in English and Spanish.

Where is my workout data stored?

On your device. An account is used only to control access during the beta. See the Privacy Policy for the details.

Do I need equipment?

No. Choose bodyweight only and every move works in an office, a hotel room or a park. Add the equipment you have and IpsoFit uses it too.

How do I join the beta?

Email support@ipsofit.com with your platform. Android testers also send the Google account they use on Google Play.

Is IpsoFit medical advice?

No. IpsoFit plans sports exercise and does not know your health. Read the Medical & Safety Disclaimer before your first block.
