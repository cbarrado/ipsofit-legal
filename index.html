IpsoFit is a Pomodoro app for Windows and Android that turns short breaks into five-minute exercise blocks, generating randomised workouts and stretching routines. Your workout data stays on your device; an account is used only to control access during the beta. These pages set out how IpsoFit handles your data, the terms of use, and the health and safety notice you accept before exercising.
