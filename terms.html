2. What IpsoFit is

IpsoFit is a Pomodoro timer for Windows and Android that turns short breaks into guided five-minute exercise blocks. It generates randomised workouts and stretching routines — short mobility and strength "snacks", longer workout sessions, stretches and eye-rest breaks — with recurring reminders and local statistics. Your workout data is stored only on your device.

IpsoFit is currently a beta: features change, some may be incomplete, and access is by approval.
