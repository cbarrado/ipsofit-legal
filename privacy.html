Privacy Policy

Version 1.4 — last updated 20 September 2026

Your workouts never leave your device. IpsoFit keeps exercise sessions, sets, statistics and settings in a local database on your computer or phone. The only personal data we hold on a server is what an account needs: your email address and your access status.

1. Who is responsible

IpsoFit is developed and operated by Carlos Barrado, an individual developer ("we", "us"). We are the data controller for the account data described in this policy. Contact: support@ipsofit.com. We are registered with the UK Information Commissioner's Office (ICO), registration number ZC249338.

2. In short

  • Workout and health-adjacent data stay on your device. We never receive them.
  • An account is required during the beta. We store your email address, how you signed in, your role and access status, and a subscription tier field that is currently unused.
  • No analytics, no advertising, no crash reporting, no tracking.
  • Our servers are hosted by Supabase in the European Union (AWS eu-west-1, Ireland).
  • You can delete your account from inside the app at any time, and export your local data on desktop.

3. Data that stays on your device

Everything IpsoFit knows about your training lives in a database file inside the app's private storage on your device: Pomodoro sessions, exercise blocks and sets, completed repetitions, muscle-group statistics, exercise calibration, equipment context, reminders and every other setting. This data is not uploaded, synchronised or backed up by us. It is removed when you uninstall the app (Android), when you use the operating system's "Clear data" (Android), or when you tick "Also erase workout data on this device" while deleting your account (desktop). The Android app opts out of Android's automatic app backup, so no copy of this data — or of your sign-in session — is kept in your Google account backup, and a reinstall starts fresh.

On desktop you can export this database to a file of your choosing (Settings → Data → Export) and import it again later. The exported file is yours: where you keep it and who you share it with is under your control.

5. Connections the app makes

  • Signing in and staying signed in — calls to our authentication provider (Supabase). Your device's IP address is visible to it, as with any internet request.
  • Google sign-in — opens Google in your browser (desktop) or in a secure browser tab (Android). Only happens if you pick that option.
  • Update check — on launch, and when you press "Check for updates", the app asks our download storage (Supabase Storage) whether a newer version exists. The request carries your IP address and the app version. On Windows installer builds the update itself is downloaded from the same storage; other builds show a download link.
  • Deleting your account — one call to our server that removes your account.

That is the complete list. IpsoFit does not send telemetry, usage statistics, crash reports or advertising identifiers anywhere.

7. How long we keep data

  • Account records — until you delete your account, or until we close it after the beta ends. Deletion removes the account, its profile and any pending request immediately.
  • Access request records — deleted when the request is approved or rejected.
  • Authentication logs — a limited period set by Supabase, after which they are purged automatically.
  • Provider backups — Supabase keeps routine database backups for a short rolling window; a deleted record disappears from them when the window expires.

8. Your rights and how to use them

Under the GDPR and similar laws you can ask for access to, rectification of, erasure of, or a copy of your personal data, ask us to restrict or object to processing, and complain to your data protection authority. Most of it you can do yourself:

  • Erasure — in the app: Settings → Account → Delete account. If your request is still pending you will find the same option on the waiting screen. Type your email to confirm. On desktop you can also erase the local database in the same step; on Android, use the system's "Clear data" or uninstall the app to remove local data.
  • Portability of local data — desktop: Settings → Data → Export my data produces a complete copy of your local database.
  • Access, rectification, or anything else — email support@ipsofit.com from the address on your account. We answer within one month.

9. Children

IpsoFit is not directed at children. Do not create an account if you are under 16.

10. Security

All connections use TLS. Account rows are protected by row-level security so a signed-in user can read only their own record; administrative actions run through server-side functions that verify the caller. Approval links are single-use tokens. Your workout data never reaches a server, which is the strongest protection we can offer for it.

11. Changes to this policy

We will post any change here with a new version number and date. If a change materially affects what we do with your data, the app will tell you the next time you open it.
