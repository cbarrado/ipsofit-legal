Delete your IpsoFit account

Applies to the IpsoFit app for Android and Windows, developed by IpsoFit — last updated 24 October 2026

You can delete your account at any time, with or without the app. Your workouts are stored only on your device, so the account holds very little: your email address and your access status.

Option 1 — in the app (immediate)

  1. Open IpsoFit and sign in.
  2. Go to Settings → Account → Delete account. If your access request is still pending, the same option is on the waiting screen.
  3. Type your email address to confirm and press Delete account.

The account is deleted at once and you are signed out.

Option 2 — by email (no app needed)

  1. Email support@ipsofit.com with the subject "Delete my IpsoFit account", from the email address on your account.
  2. We reply to that address to confirm the request comes from you. Answer the reply to confirm.
  3. We delete the account within 30 days of your confirmation, usually much sooner, and email you when it is done.

We never ask for your password. If you no longer have access to the account's email address, tell us in your message and we will agree another way to confirm it is you.

What is deleted

  • Your account: email address, account identifier, sign-in method and, for Google sign-in, the name and profile picture URL Google shared.
  • Your profile: role, access status and subscription tier.
  • Any pending access request.

What is kept, and for how long

  • Feedback you sent from the app, with the email address it was sent with — kept until it is no longer needed to fix or improve the app. Ask in the same email and we delete it too.
  • Authentication logs — a limited period set by our hosting provider (Supabase), then purged automatically.
  • Provider backups — a deleted account disappears from the routine database backups when their short rolling window expires.

Data on your device

Workouts, statistics and settings never reach our servers, so deleting the account does not touch them. To remove them: on Android, uninstall the app or use the system's "Clear data"; on Windows, tick "Also erase workout data on this device" when you delete the account in the app.
